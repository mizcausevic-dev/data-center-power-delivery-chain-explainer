# Safeguards

Status: conceptual brief. Not a certified engineering design. Not construction documents. Not operating, testing, bypass, or maintenance instructions.

## What this film refuses to show

- A transfer to static bypass as the response to losing the input. Bypass needs a suitable source. Stored-energy support is the inverter staying on the DC link.
- An unlimited battery. The authored store falls during the interruption and is still finite at the hold. Recharge is not shown as complete.
- Universal redundancy. One module is the boundary.
- A startup, connection, charge, or switching procedure.
- Precise dimensions, voltages, currents, efficiencies, runtimes, or certified performance.
- A measured or simulated graph. The trace is qualitative and authored.
- Electron arrows on AC. Markers are energy flow.
- Live electrical work.

## Reference boundary

Starting point: U.S. Department of Energy, Best Practices Guide for Energy-Efficient Data Center Design, July 2024, https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/best-practice-guide-data-center-design.pdf

Verify geometry, modes, and any publication claim against the documentation for the selected equipment.
