Safeguards

Conceptual explanation of one chosen boundary. Not a certified design, not construction documents, and not instructions for operating, testing, bypassing, or maintaining equipment. Nothing here is a measurement. The energy trace and waveforms are qualitative. No runtime, redundancy, efficiency, voltage, or dimension is claimed. Verify any real installation against the documentation for the selected equipment. Starting point: the U.S. Department of Energy Best Practices Guide for Energy-Efficient Data Center Design, July 2024. This page is not a DOE publication.
